Research direction · Biological Crossroads

Following biological mechanisms across disorders

Why can different biological defects produce overlapping tissue vulnerabilities, while similar pathway disruptions sometimes produce very different outcomes?

This investigation explores research on RPIA deficiency, transaldolase deficiency, Sanfilippo syndrome, redox regulation, cellular stress and tissue-specific response. The interest is in mechanisms: which molecular step changes, how the disturbance propagates, and what observations distinguish one explanation from another.

A recurring methodological rule is that molecular location does not by itself prove transport route or function, and similarity between disease outcomes does not establish shared causation. A curated source bibliography and public-safe full documents can be added as they are verified.

Research direction · Cognition

Model construction, imagery, and analogy

This self-observation project distinguishes the automatic recognition of relationships from later deliberate checking. Topics include visuospatial mental imagery, spatial perspective-taking, mental model construction, mechanistic and analogical reasoning, and metacognitive revision.

The practical research question is how to translate subjective reports about thinking into clear, testable questions without confusing phenomenology with measured performance.

Research method

From observation to testable claim

The work distinguishes observed information from interpretations and explicitly records what could challenge a preferred explanation.

01
Observe

Record what is known, by whom, and under what conditions.

02
Model

Specify proposed mechanisms, levels, and arrows of influence.

03
Compare

Consult published findings, competing hypotheses, and possible confounders.

04
Test

Define measurable predictions and evaluate them against independent data.

Scientific disclosure: These are developing conceptual syntheses and research questions, not validated theories, diagnostic tools, clinical guidance, or evidence of newly established universal laws.
