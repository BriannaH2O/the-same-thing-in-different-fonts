How to move toward predictive science

Define the focal population and a measurable outcome. Specify exposure, controls, units, time interval, and uncertainty. Pre-record predictions; compare with held-out observations; benchmark against simpler competing models. Cross-domain similarities may be analogies rather than common causal machinery. The workbench does not generate numerical predictions until domain-specific equations and data are provided.

How to grow this single framework

Each lens is a set of nested-process definitions and candidate measures. Add new lenses without changing the original four domains or six-pair structure. Biological evolution and individual development require different time units; tectonic systems are an environmental application rather than living organisms.
